Introduction

Introduction

Heavy-flavor hadron decays provide an important laboratory for studying exotic hadronic structures beyond the conventional quark model. In particular, the invariant mass spectrum of the system in exclusive decays has attracted considerable attention due to the observation of resonance-like structures near threshold.

In this work, we perform a reconstruction and analysis of the decay chain

with subsequent decays

Using CMS Open Data together with modern open-source analysis frameworks, we reproduce the full analysis workflow including event reconstruction, candidate selection, invariant mass fitting, background suppression, and the study of the mass spectrum.

Particular attention is devoted to the extraction of clean candidates and to the investigation of possible structures in the invariant mass distribution. The analysis includes studies of signal modeling, efficiency corrections, sideband subtraction, and robustness checks against selection variations.

Beyond the physics motivation itself, this project also demonstrates how complex heavy-flavor analyses can be reproduced in a fully open and reproducible environment using publicly available collider data.
